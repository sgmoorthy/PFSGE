Mathematical Modeling & Theoretical Principles

Physical governance of DNA reptation mitigation in graded matrices

1. Overdamped Langevin Migration Dynamics
ζ(r) (dx/dt) = q E(t) + η(t)

Where q is effective segment charge, E(t) = E₀(cos ωt, sin ωt) is the rotating field vector, and η(t) denotes Brownian noise satisfying ⟨η_i(t)η_j(t')⟩ = 2D₀/ζ(r) δ_ij δ(t-t').

2. Radial Drag (Porosity) Gradient
ζ(r) = ζ₀ (1 + α r²)

Progressive pore shrinkage radially outward prevents reptation elongation and band bunching for fragments > 5 Mb.

3. Archimedean Deposition Mechanics
r(θ) = r_inner + (w / 2π) θ

Toolpath coordinates ensuring uniform bead width w and constant linear casting speed via regulated rotation ω_stage(r) = v_linear / r.

4. Radial Density & Virtual Electropherograms
r_i = √(x_i² + y_i²),   σ_r² = (1/N) Σ (r_i - r̄)²

Direct radial projection maps 2D spiral migration bands into quantifiable 1D electropherograms with minimal band broadening.
